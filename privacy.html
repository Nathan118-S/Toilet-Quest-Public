Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026 · Applies to the Toilet Quest iPhone and iPad app and the Toilet Quest App Clip

At a glance

  • We don't collect your data. Toilet Quest has no servers of its own, and the developer can't see your bathrooms, ratings or account.
  • Your data is stored on your device and, if you sign in, in your own private iCloud account. Most of it is end-to-end encrypted.
  • Your bathrooms are shared only with friends you approve, or with people you send a share link to.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • You can delete everything at any time in the app: Profile ▸ Delete Account.

Who we are

Toilet Quest is developed by Nathan Sandy ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email support@nathan-net.com.

iCloud and encryption

When you're signed in, Toilet Quest uses Apple's CloudKit to store your data in your private iCloud database, which belongs to your iCloud account. The app never uses a public database. The details of your bathrooms, ratings and profile are stored in CloudKit's encrypted fields, which are end-to-end encrypted with keys from your iCloud account, and photos are stored as encrypted iCloud assets. We cannot access your private iCloud data. This storage is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing with friends

  • Only after both people accept. When you and a friend accept each other's request, you can see each other's bathrooms, ratings, display name, emoji and profile photo through iCloud sharing.
  • Invite codes and links. Your QR code or invite link lets the person you give it to send you a friend request. A request contains their display name, emoji and the links needed to connect. Anyone who has your invite link may also be able to see other pending requests sent to you, so only share it with people you know.
  • Removing a friend immediately removes their bathrooms from your map and stops them from seeing yours.

Share links and the App Clip

When you tap Share Link on a bathroom, the link itself contains that bathroom's name, location, ratings and amenities, and your display name. Anyone who receives the link can open it in the Toilet Quest App Clip or the app, without an account. We don't receive or store these links. Only share a bathroom with people you're happy to show it to. The App Clip only displays the shared bathroom; it doesn't collect or store any information.

Third parties

Toilet Quest contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and we don't sell or rent data. The app relies only on Apple services: Sign in with Apple, iCloud/CloudKit, and Apple Maps for the map and directions. Apple Maps may receive your location to show the map and give directions, as described in Apple's Privacy Policy. When you share a bathroom, it goes to the apps you choose (for example Messages), under their own policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete Account (Profile ▸ Delete Account) erases all Toilet Quest data on your device and in your iCloud, removes you from your friends' maps, and signs you out.
  • Exit Guest Mode deletes bathrooms saved as a guest.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your device; data in your iCloud stays until you delete your account in the app.
  • To stop using your Apple ID with Toilet Quest, go to Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Sign in with Apple.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you have rights to access, correct, delete and move your personal data. Because your data is held on your device and in your own iCloud account, you can exercise these rights directly in the app: view and edit your bathrooms and profile, or delete everything with Delete Account. If you need help, contact us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

Toilet Quest is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app doesn't send personal information to us, we hold no such information.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Toilet Quest handles data, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
