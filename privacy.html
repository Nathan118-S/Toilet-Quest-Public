Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · Applies to the Toilet Quest iPhone and iPad app and the Toilet Quest App Clip

At a glance

  • We don't collect your data. Toilet Quest has no servers of its own, and the developer can't see your bathrooms, ratings or account.
  • Your data is stored on your device and, if you sign in, backed up through Apple's iCloud. It's encrypted on your device first, so neither we nor anyone else can read it, and it doesn't use your iCloud storage.
  • Your bathrooms are shared only with friends you approve, or with people you send a share link to.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • You can delete everything at any time in the app: Profile ▸ Delete Account.

Who we are

Toilet Quest is developed by Nathan Sandy ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy or your data, email support@nathan-net.com.

iCloud and encryption

When you're signed in, Toilet Quest stores your data with Apple's CloudKit, in the app's own iCloud database. This doesn't use your iCloud storage, so it works even when your iCloud is full. Before anything leaves your device, the app encrypts it with keys that only you have (bathrooms, ratings, profile and photo are also readable by friends you accept; door codes only by you). The keys are kept in your iCloud Keychain, which Apple end-to-end encrypts. Neither we nor Apple nor other users can read your encrypted data.

To make syncing and friend connections work, a few technical details are stored without encryption: a random ID for your account that's specific to this app (it isn't your Apple ID, name or email), which of these IDs are connected as friends or have sent a request, your public encryption key, how many items you've stored, and when they were changed. Other Toilet Quest users could technically see these details for an ID they know, but not anything you've written. This storage is provided by Apple and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing with friends

  • Only after both people accept. When you and a friend accept each other's request, your apps exchange keys so you can see each other's bathrooms, ratings, display name, emoji and profile photo. Nobody else can read them.
  • Invite codes and links. Your QR code or invite link contains your account's random app ID and the display name and emoji you chose, so the person you give it to can send you a friend request. Only share it with people you know.
  • Removing a friend immediately removes their bathrooms from your map. Your app also switches to new encryption keys, so they can't see anything you add or change afterwards.

Share links and the App Clip

When you tap Share Link on a bathroom, the link itself contains that bathroom's name, location, ratings and amenities, and your display name. Anyone who receives the link can open it in the Toilet Quest App Clip or the app, without an account. We don't receive or store these links. Only share a bathroom with people you're happy to show it to. The App Clip only displays the shared bathroom; it doesn't collect or store any information.

Third parties

Toilet Quest contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and we don't sell or rent data. The app relies only on Apple services: Sign in with Apple, iCloud/CloudKit, and Apple Maps for the map and directions. Apple Maps may receive your location to show the map and give directions, as described in Apple's Privacy Policy. When you share a bathroom, it goes to the apps you choose (for example Messages), under their own policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete Account (Profile ▸ Delete Account) erases all Toilet Quest data on your device and everything you've stored in iCloud through the app, including your keys, removes you from your friends' maps, and signs you out.
  • Exit Guest Mode deletes bathrooms saved as a guest.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your device; your encrypted backup stays in iCloud until you delete your account in the app.
  • To stop using your Apple ID with Toilet Quest, go to Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Sign in with Apple.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you have rights to access, correct, delete and move your personal data. Because your data is held on your device and encrypted with your own keys, you can exercise these rights directly in the app: view and edit your bathrooms and profile, or delete everything with Delete Account. If you need help, contact us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

Toilet Quest is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app doesn't send personal information to us, we hold no such information.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Toilet Quest handles data, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
