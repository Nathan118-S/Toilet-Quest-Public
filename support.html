Getting started

How do I add a bathroom?
  1. Tap the blue + button on the map.
  2. Drag the pin (or tap the map) to the exact spot.
  3. Enter a name, like “Target – front entrance”.
  4. Choose the amenities, rate Cleanliness, Privacy and Accessibility, and tap Save.
Do I need an account?

No. Tap Continue Without Signing In to use Toilet Quest as a guest. Guest bathrooms are saved on your iPhone only, so they aren't backed up, and you can't add friends. Sign in with Apple at any time to back them up; everything you added as a guest comes with you.

What do the pin colors mean?

Pins are colored by overall rating: green 4 stars or more, yellow 3 or more, orange 2 or more, red below 2, and gray not rated yet. A small avatar on a pin means a friend added it, and a purple diamond marks a hidden gem.

How do I find a specific kind of bathroom?

Use the search bar to find a bathroom by name; results are sorted nearest first. Tap the filter button next to it to show only bathrooms that are unlocked, hidden gems, have a bidet or baby changing table, meet a minimum rating, and more.

Friends and sharing

How do I add a friend?
  1. Both of you need to be signed in with Apple and to iCloud.
  2. One person opens Friends ▸ Add Friend ▸ My Code.
  3. The other opens Scan Code and scans it, or opens the invite link sent with Share Invite Link, then sends the request.
  4. The first person taps Accept. Your bathrooms then appear on each other's maps.

Nothing is shared until the request is accepted.

My friend request isn't arriving
  • Make sure the two phones are signed in to different iCloud accounts, with iCloud Drive turned on.
  • Open the Friends screen and pull down to refresh.
  • Check that both phones have the latest version of Toilet Quest.
  • If you see “friend code wasn't found”, ask your friend to close and reopen My Code, then scan again.
How do I share a bathroom with someone who doesn't have the app?

Open the bathroom, tap the Share button in the top right, then Share Link. The link opens instantly in the Toilet Quest App Clip with the ratings, amenities and directions, with no download needed. If they already have the app, it opens there and asks whether to add the bathroom. You can also choose Share as Image.

How do I remove a friend?

In Friends, swipe left on their name and tap Remove. Their bathrooms disappear from your map and they can no longer see yours.

iCloud and your data

My bathrooms aren't syncing
  • Check you're signed in to iCloud: Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud, with iCloud Drive on.
  • Look at the status under the map title. “Offline” means changes will sync when you're back online.
  • In the app, open Profile ▸ iCloud Diagnostics and tap Check Again. Any problem is listed with a suggested fix.
  • Still stuck? Tap Copy Report and email it to us.
Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone and, if you're signed in, in your own private iCloud, where most of it is end-to-end encrypted. We don't run any servers and can't see your data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Profile (your avatar, top left) ▸ Delete Account. This erases your bathrooms, ratings, friends and profile from your iPhone and your iCloud, and removes you from your friends' maps. It can't be undone.

Guests can tap Profile ▸ Exit Guest Mode to delete bathrooms saved on the device.

The map doesn't show where I am

Allow location access: Settings ▸ Toilet Quest ▸ Location ▸ While Using the App. Toilet Quest only uses your location on your device, to center the map and place new bathrooms.
